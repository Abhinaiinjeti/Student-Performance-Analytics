Why is the PySpark Monitor in EduPulse? (Distributed Big Data Engine)
System Tools & Benchmark Suite

EduPulse Analytics operates with a dual-engine architecture designed for both everyday classroom marksheets and high-throughput university scale:

Standard Academic Workflows (Excel & Marksheets): For regular departmental operations (100–5,000 students), multi-semester grade sheets, laboratory marks, and mid-exams, EduPulse uses the Universal In-Memory Engine (via the Import Wizard and Academic Analysis) for instant, zero-delay results.
PySpark Distributed Engine (This Monitor): This dedicated backend is used for asynchronous processing of massive datasets (10,000 to 1,000,000+ student records), multi-node parallel aggregations, and generating synthetic benchmark stress-tests. It is kept running as an enterprise service to guarantee zero latency on large institutional workloads.
