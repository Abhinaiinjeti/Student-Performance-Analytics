Core Analytical Architecture

Two Distinct Academic Data Models

EduPulse never conflates letter grades with raw marks. We preserve authentic academic semantics across institutional paradigms.

Model A

Continuous Marks-Based Results

Designed for autonomous colleges and internal assessments featuring modular evaluation components:

  • Internal Marks + External Theory Exam components
  • Per-subject configurable maximum & passing cutoffs
  • Explicit non-numeric states: PASS, FAIL, ABSENT, WITHHELD
  • Interactive Class Marks spreadsheet entry with draft persistence
  • Standard deviation, median, and difficulty index analytics
Primary formats: Spreadsheet Matrix, CSV, Direct Entry
Model B

University Grade-Based Results

Engineered for university result sheets with categorical letter grades and SGPA metrics:

  • Discrete Letter Grades: O, A+, A, B+, B, C, P, F
  • Preserves official SGPA as an authoritative institutional metric
  • Distinct status preservation: Ab (Absent) vs Blank vs Failed
  • Strict guardrail: No unapproved numeric conversion of grades
  • Grade frequency distribution curves & pass ratios
Primary formats: University Tabular Sheets, Multi-Sheet XLSX

Technical Foundations

Enterprise Big Data Architecture Without Enterprise Complexity

EduPulse Analytics combines the simplicity of a local web application with the immense power of Apache PySpark. Heavy transformations are completely decoupled from web requests, providing sub-second responsiveness even under million-record workloads.
